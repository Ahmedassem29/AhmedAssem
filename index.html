AI video

AI shots with a compositor's finish

Generation gets an idea on screen fast. Compositing makes it hold up on air. I use both, and that mix is part of the promo work that won seven Telly Awards in 2025.

  1. Frame1 Step 1

    Frame

    Key frames, looks and characters, with faces kept consistent from shot to shot.

    Nano Banana Pro · Higgsfield Soul

  2. Motion1 Step 2

    Motion

    Frames become shots through image-to-video and text-to-video. End frames chain into the next shot for continuous one-take moves.

    Seedance · Kling

  3. Merge1 Step 3

    Composite

    AI plates meet live action and graphics: keying, rotoscoping, tracking and clean-up.

    Nuke · After Effects · Mocha

  4. Write1 Step 4

    Finish

    Edit and color, then delivery to broadcast and social specs.

    Premiere Pro

About

Behind the frame

I'm Ahmed Assem, a promo producer and VFX compositor based in Dubai.

I started in 2010 at a small animation studio in Cairo, before I graduated. I went on to composite feature films, TV series and commercials at AROMA Studios and Squids Visual Arts, then moved into the promo departments of Al Nahar, DMC, CNBC Arabia, Al Arabiya and Al Mashhad.

I cover the whole chain: idea, shoot, design, compositing and finish. That range is what lets me take AI-generated footage and make it sit next to live action on air.
